What it does

01

Record

Start it and drive. Revly draws the route live, keeps the numbers steady on screen, and stays out of the way for the rest of the journey.

  • Live map with your route drawn as you go
  • Speed, distance, duration and elevation as you drive
  • Lock Screen Live Activity, so the phone can stay face down
  • Pause and resume without splitting the drive in two

02

Garage

Every car you own, kept properly. Photos, details, and the drives that belong to each one — so a car has a history instead of a spec sheet.

  • Multiple vehicles with photos and details
  • Per-vehicle stats and drive history
  • Cars gain levels as you put distance on them

03

Progress

What you did this week, this month, this year — answered without you doing arithmetic.

  • Weekly distance and speed trends as charts
  • Streaks for driving regularly
  • Achievements and levels that track real distance

04

People

A feed of drives, not opinions. Follow people who drive what you like, and go out together when it matters.

  • A feed built around drives and vehicles
  • Clubs for the people you actually ride with
  • Convoys with live location, shared only inside the convoy

05

Exploration

The map fills in behind you. Cities and countries you have driven through, recorded as you pass through them.

  • Cities and countries unlocked by driving them
  • A record of where you have actually been

06

On every Apple screen you own

Revly is a native iOS app, and it behaves like one everywhere the platform lets it.

  • Apple Watch companion
  • CarPlay
  • Live Activities and Lock Screen widgets
  • Works offline — drives record without a signal and sync later
  • Export any drive as GPX

Where we stand

A driving app that records where you go owes you a straight answer.

Location, only while it is doing something
A driving journal cannot work without precise location, so Revly asks for it. What it does with it changes by situation: nothing while you browse, a lighter mode for the map, and continuous only while you are recording a drive or sharing a convoy.
Convoy location stays inside the convoy
Live location is visible to the members of a convoy you joined, and membership is enforced on the server — an app cannot add itself to a convoy to start reading positions.
No time rankings on public roads
Revly does not rank drives by how fast you covered a public road. A leaderboard like that is an incentive to speed on a road with other people on it. Lap boards exist for circuits, because a circuit is a different place with different rules.
Your drives leave with you
Export any drive as a GPX file, and delete your account — with its data — from inside the app. Neither one requires emailing us for permission.
